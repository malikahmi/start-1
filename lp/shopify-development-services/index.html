FAQ

Questions before you send it

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The audit and the follow-up call cost nothing, and there is no obligation to hire us afterwards.

What do you look at in my store?

Page speed on mobile, your homepage, collection and product pages, the path to checkout, and the basics of how your store shows up in search. You get the findings ranked by likely impact, so you know what to fix first.

Do you only work on Shopify?

Shopify is our focus, including Shopify Plus. We also build on WooCommerce and WordPress and ship custom sites when a store is not the right fit.

How much will it cost to fix what you find?

That depends on scope. A speed fix and a full rebuild are very different jobs. We quote in writing after the audit, before any work starts.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to reply about your audit and nothing else. Read the full privacy policy.
